# Planning review: Mandapam → CNI Civil Lines → CPI

## Overall assessment

The supplied checklist is strong on church preparation, outfits, purchases and reception ideas. Its biggest operational gap is the coordination of **three properties**, overnight guests and pre-wedding functions. The original website also mixed sample information with confirmed facts and claimed Google synchronization before a dependable shared save existed.

The upgraded planner keeps the useful detailed checklist, starts guest lists and prices empty, and adds 27 bilingual operations tasks. Suggested schedules and optional customs are identified as drafts. Nothing on a template establishes a booking.

## Decide first

1. **Date and church slot:** written church confirmation, actual reporting time, pastor-approved service order, counselling/rehearsal dates, document list, witnesses, notice/banns where applicable, officiant authorization and certificate/registration route. Confirm the current requirements with the church and appropriate Bilaspur authority.
2. **Mandapam contract:** exact halls/lawn, functions, room inventory, check-in/out, beds, breakfast and every meal, cleaning/stain charges, sound cutoff, power and cancellation terms.
3. **CPI contract:** exact property, hall and entrance; package inclusions, GST, guaranteed pax, final count deadline, extra-plate price, overtime and dinner service rules.
4. **Budget ceiling and decision authority:** name who signs off changes and who pays. Count every package once; separate quotes from agreed commitments and all paid instalments from outstanding balances.
5. **Named people:** one overall lead, one coordinator per family, a lead at each venue, and named transport, RSVP, payments and emergency leads with backups.

## Practical use

- In the first family meeting, assign the urgent tasks and exact deadlines. Not every purchase is urgent; mark irrelevant items not applicable so progress stays meaningful.
- Review outstanding decisions, blocked tasks, due payments and uncertain RSVPs weekly. In the final week, reconfirm vendors, meals, arrivals and transfers daily.
- Measure the actual transfer at the relevant time of day and add boarding/elderly assistance time. Do not turn template travel times into promises.
- Finalize active events and IST dates before Calendar publishing. Individual calendar-copy links do not update automatically when the master event changes.
- Before wedding week, verify the family link on two phones, save a Drive snapshot and download a JSON backup. Print the current roles/contact, schedule, guest-travel and backup tabs for on-ground leads.

Venue addresses, phone numbers and map searches are inherited references, not independently verified current confirmations. Verify the exact property, manager and entrance before paying vendors or sending directions.
