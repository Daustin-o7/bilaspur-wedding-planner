The little details.

Edit both languages below. Save a local draft to preview it, then download settings.js, replace the file in this folder and redeploy to publish your changes.

नीचे दोनों भाषाओं में विवरण बदलें। पूर्वावलोकन के लिए स्थानीय प्रारूप सहेजें। प्रकाशित करने के लिए settings.js डाउनलोड करें, इस फ़ोल्डर की फ़ाइल बदलें और साइट दोबारा अपलोड करें।

This editor saves only to your browser / केवल ब्राउज़र में सहेजता है।

It does not change the live website. Keep private guest lists, budgets, passwords and service keys out of these public settings. Open the site through the preview server for reliable draft previews.

All dates and times remain blank until you enter them. Verify the Hindi spelling of both names with the couple. Event confirmed enables calendar downloads when a date, start time and end time are present. All times use IST; end times must be on the same day.

New sections: event attire also fills the Dress Code guide. Accommodation selects a venue ID; leave it blank to hide the section. Edit Gifts & blessings in both languages, or clear the note to hide it. The paired portraits and swipe gallery reuse your three Photos paths.

Import or reset / आयात या रीसेट

Import a JSON backup created here. Reset discards only this editor’s local draft and restores the deployed settings.js.

Photos, voice & RSVP

Photos: put replacement JPG, PNG or WebP files in assets/, then edit their paths in the Photos section. The supplied original photograph remains at assets/couple.jpg; the current portraits are assets/hero-portrait.jpg, assets/story-portrait.jpg and assets/closing-portrait.jpg. photos.hero supplies the arched portrait; photos.story supplies the framed story photo; photos.closing supplies the closing cameo.

Music: the floating speaker starts or mutes music. Leave audio.music blank for the gentle synthesized melody, or put your own recording in assets/ and set audio.music to its path, for example assets/music.mp3. Scrolling does not restart sound. MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A and AAC paths are supported.

स्पीकर छूने पर संगीत चलता या बंद होता है। संगीत वाले भाग में audio.music खाली छोड़ने पर हल्का संगीत बजता है; अपनी रिकॉर्डिंग के लिए फ़ाइल assets/ में रखें और उसका पथ भरें। नीचे स्क्रॉल करने से संगीत दोबारा शुरू नहीं होता।

Free RSVP: create a Google Form with bilingual questions and link its responses to a Google Sheet. Set mode to link and paste its responder URL into formUrl. Keep the response Sheet restricted to the family. Test the form signed out and check that a response arrives. off shows “opening soon”; netlify is only for an existing Netlify Forms deployment.

निःशुल्क उपस्थिति व्यवस्था के लिए गूगल फ़ॉर्म बनाएँ, उत्तर गूगल शीट से जोड़ें, मोड link चुनें और formUrl में अतिथियों वाला लिंक डालें।

Free publishing: upload this forest folder or forest.zip to Cloudflare Pages on the Free plan. Use its included pages.dev address for WhatsApp and link sharing. Settings, photos and recordings live in that same deployment. The opening card uses your hero photograph; draft previews skip the opening. Changes here affect only this design; copy your exported settings and any added assets into wine to update both. See Invite/README.md for the full guide.
